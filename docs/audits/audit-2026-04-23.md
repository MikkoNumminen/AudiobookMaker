## Recommended next steps
1. Wrap the two `NamedTemporaryFile(delete=False)` calls at [src/synthesis_orchestrator.py:383,404](src/synthesis_orchestrator.py#L383) in a try/finally that deletes them on any failure — same fix handles the dominant partial-file and orphaned-temp-file findings in one place.
2. Add explicit `timeout=` to the three unbounded network/subprocess waits most likely to hang the GUI: [src/auto_updater.py:362](src/auto_updater.py#L362), [src/tts_piper.py:362](src/tts_piper.py#L362), [src/engine_installer_voice_cloner.py:156-171](src/engine_installer_voice_cloner.py#L156-L171).
3. Guard the shared booleans `_synth_running` / `_cancel_requested` in [src/gui_unified.py:484-486](src/gui_unified.py#L484-L486) with a `threading.Event` (or a single lock) to collapse the cluster of race findings around lines 763, 1740, 2093, 3125.
4. In [src/voice_recorder.py:523](src/voice_recorder.py#L523), move `self._recording = True` to after the `Popen` assignment succeeds, and put the process handle on `self` inside the same try/except — removes the inconsistent-state window.
5. Reject the first-chunk-wins contract in [src/tts_audio.py:147,204](src/tts_audio.py#L147) — validate that every chunk matches `(rate, channels, sample_width)` and raise with a clear error instead of silently resampling; prevents whole-audiobook corruption from a single bad chunk.
